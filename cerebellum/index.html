About this view

The base is OME channel 1 (DAPI) from the full-resolution image. The colored layer is the masked 30 px SpaceRec Stage 1 Top-1 grid type map. Both layers use the same 20,296 × 20,296 coordinate frame and are displayed at 3,200 × 3,200.
